Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🔒 Are my files uploaded to the internet or any server?

No, absolutely not. All file compression, resizing, PDF merging, and conversions are executed 100% locally on your device. Your files never leave your phone.

📄 Which PDF tools are included?

FileEase includes Convert Photos/Images to PDF, Merge multiple PDFs, Split PDF pages, Compress PDF size, Password Lock/Unlock, and Rotate or Reorder pages.

🖼️ What image formats are supported?

JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and WebP (.webp). You can compress images in batch or resize them to custom dimensions.

🎬 Can I compress videos?

Yes, the video compressor allows you to significantly reduce video file size while maintaining clarity, making it easy to send via email or WhatsApp.

📱 Supported Operating Systems

FileEase supports iOS 12.0 and later (iPhone and iPad) as well as Android 8.0 and later.
