Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the "Safe to Spend Today" calculation work?

The Safe to Spend Today metric is Monetra's proprietary deterministic financial gauge. Instead of showing retroactive past totals, it calculates how much discretionary money you can safely spend today without risking your upcoming obligations:

Safe to Spend = (Total Budget - Month-to-date Spending - Committed Recurring Bills) / Days Remaining in Month

This ensures that rent, bills, and savings goals are mathematically protected before you spend.

Is Monetra connected to my bank account or online credentials?

No. Monetra is built on a strict zero bank sync, 100% offline-first architecture. You never share your online banking passwords, account numbers, or card details. All data is logged and processed privately on your phone's local SQLite database.

Which languages are supported and how do I switch?

Monetra natively supports 6 languages with full RTL layout support:

  • 🇺🇸 English (Default)
  • 🇫🇷 Français (French)
  • 🇸🇦 العربية (Arabic - RTL)
  • 🇩🇪 Deutsch (German)
  • 🇪🇸 Español (Spanish)
  • 🇧🇷 Português (Portuguese)

To change the language: Navigate to Settings > Language and tap your preferred language flag.

How do I backup or export my financial data?

You can export your complete history anytime:

  • Go to Settings > Data Management.
  • Select Export to CSV (for opening in Excel / Google Sheets) or Export to JSON (for complete backups).
  • Save the exported file to your device or personal cloud storage.
How does Biometric Lock (Fingerprint / Face ID) work?

When you enable App Lock in Settings, Monetra invokes the Android BiometricPrompt security dialog. Authentication is verified in your phone's hardware Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). Monetra never has access to your raw biometrics.

How can I reset or permanently delete all my data?

Because Monetra does not store anything on remote cloud servers, you have complete instant control:

  • Inside the app: Go to Settings > Reset Data and confirm.
  • Alternatively, clearing the app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently erases the local database.
