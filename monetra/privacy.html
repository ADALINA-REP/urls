Privacy Policy

Monetra: Budget & Expenses (com.monetra.budget.expense.tracker)
Last Updated & Effective Date: November 2, 2026

🛡️ Privacy-First & Local-Only Architecture

Monetra is engineered with a strict local-first privacy model. Your financial entries, expense logs, budget targets, and recurring subscriptions are stored strictly locally on your physical device. We do not require bank login credentials, do not link to third-party bank scrapers, and do not transmit or sell your financial records to remote servers.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ADALINA / SISCOM ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data when you install and use Monetra: Budget & Expenses (the "Application" or "App").

We respect your fundamental right to financial privacy. Unlike conventional personal finance applications that monetize or profile user banking behavior, Monetra is deliberately architected as an offline-first manual tracker and analytical planner that operates without cloud tracking of your private finances.

2. Summary of Data Practices

Category What Is Collected Where It Is Processed / Stored
Financial Data Expenses, incomes, categories, notes, custom budget thresholds. 100% On-Device in encrypted/private SQLite database. Never transmitted to our servers.
Bank Credentials None. We never ask for bank accounts, card numbers, or logins. Zero Collection.
Biometrics Fingerprint / Face ID for biometric app lock. Secure Enclave / TEE of your device via Android BiometricPrompt. The App never sees or stores biometric templates.
Diagnostics & Stability Anonymous crash metrics (OS version, device model, call stack). Aggregated and pseudonymized to diagnose app performance. No personal or financial details.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure total clarity regarding our compliance with Google Play Financial Services Policies and global data privacy regulations:

  • No Online Banking Credentials: We never integrate Plaid, Yodlee, MX, or automated scrapers. You retain full control over your inputs.
  • No Government IDs or Credit Scores: We do not ask for SSN, National ID, credit scores, or date of birth.
  • No Location Tracking: Monetra does not access GPS or coarse/fine location services.
  • No Contact List or Photo Scans: We do not read contacts, camera roll, or device media.
  • No Advertising ID Profiling: We do not track you across other applications or websites.

4. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

All data you create inside Monetra is stored locally within the application's isolated sandboxed directory on your device. Android OS security sandboxing prevents other installed apps from accessing Monetra's SQLite database.

If you enable App Lock, access is guarded using the Android BiometricPrompt API or device PIN. Biometric authentication is handled strictly by the device's hardware-backed Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). Monetra receives only a cryptographic boolean confirmation (success or failure) and never has access to your raw fingerprint or facial scan.

5. Data Backup, Export & Portability

You retain 100% ownership and portability of your financial information:

  • JSON / CSV Export: You can export your full transaction history and budget limits at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Manual Backup: Backups are saved directly to your chosen local folder or personal storage. We never upload your backups to unauthorized clouds.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can reset all application data at any time via Settings > Data Management > Clear All Data, or by simply uninstalling the application from your device.

6. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The Application may use standard operating system libraries (such as Google Play Services) solely for delivering updates, verifying Google Play licenses, or recording anonymized crash logs to fix bugs. These services do not receive or inspect your financial transactions, income, or budget allocations.

7. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

For users located in the EEA or UK under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Legal Basis: Data entered into the app is processed solely to perform the services requested by you. Because processing is local to your device, you act as the primary controller of your own financial entries.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, port, and erase your data at any time directly through the app interface.

8. CCPA / CPRA Compliance (California)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than performing the services reasonably expected by consumers.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Monetra is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate corrective action.
