Effective Date: November 18, 2026  •  Last Updated: November 25, 2026  •  Package ID: com.nexora.applauncher.homescreen

On-Device Privacy Architecture

Nexora is designed as a privacy-first Android homescreen launcher. Your app layouts, themes, hidden apps, folders, and launch statistics remain locally on your device. Nexora includes no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers, and requires no user account.

This Privacy Policy explains how ADALINA / SISCOM (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles information when you use Nexora: App Launcher & Themes (package com.nexora.applauncher.homescreen). By installing or using Nexora, you agree to the practices described in this document.

1. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

Nexora stores all launcher customization and personalization state strictly inside your device’s private app storage (SharedPreferences and app cache directory cacheDir):

  • Installed Applications & Icon Packs: Nexora queries launchable activities (ACTION_MAIN / CATEGORY_LAUNCHER / CATEGORY_HOME) and optional third-party icon packs (ADW, Nova, Go Launcher formats) exclusively to display apps on your homescreen, dock, folders, and app drawer. Rendered icons are cached locally in cacheDir for smooth scrolling performance and never leave your device.
  • Homescreen Layouts & Customization: Your chosen grid dimensions, minimalist home layout toggle, folders, hidden-apps list, optional 4-digit hidden-apps PIN, favorite Theme Store presets, and active glass/blur/color styling are stored locally.
  • Local Launch Counts & Manual Backups: Local app launch counts used for smart sorting and JSON configuration snapshots generated in Settings → Backup & Restore remain under your control on your device unless you explicitly share or copy the exported JSON text.

2. Permissions & Sensitive API Disclosures

Nexora works as a full launcher without enabling sensitive permissions. Optional system permissions below are requested only when you choose to activate their corresponding feature, accompanied by an in-app disclosure:

Permission / Service Requirement Purpose & Strict Privacy Guarantee
AccessibilityService API
BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE		 Optional Used solely to perform global system actions when you double-tap to lock the screen or swipe down to expand the notification shade. Configured with canRetrieveWindowContent="false": Nexora never reads screen text, passwords, keystrokes, or window contents, and never collects, stores, or shares accessibility data off-device.
Notification Access
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE		 Optional Used solely to count active non-ongoing notifications per app package so unread numeric badges can appear on app icons and folders. Notification titles, message content, and sender identities are never read, stored, or shared.
App Usage Access
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS		 Optional Used optionally to rank your most frequently used apps inside the Smart Dock and App Drawer Suggested strip. Usage statistics are evaluated 100% locally and never uploaded.
Installed App Queries
<queries> (Launcher & Icon Packs)		 Core Launcher Function Used strictly on-device to render your installed app list, app shortcuts, and supported third-party icon packs. App inventory lists are never sold, profiled, or transmitted to servers.
Wallpaper & Uninstall
SET_WALLPAPER, REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES, EXPAND_STATUS_BAR		 Action-Triggered Used only when you tap Apply Theme with UHD wallpaper enabled, tap Uninstall in an app’s menu (launching Android’s own confirmation dialog), or swipe down to open the status bar.

3. Network Requests & Third-Party Services

  • Weather Widget (Open-Meteo): When you enable the Weather widget or search for a city, Nexora uses INTERNET permission solely to contact Open-Meteo (https://api.open-meteo.com and https://geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). Only the city name you enter or the geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude) of your selected city are sent. Nexora never attaches device identifiers, Advertising IDs, or personal profiles. The latest weather forecast is cached locally for offline viewing. Review Open-Meteo Terms.
  • Bundled Typography (Offline): All app UI fonts are bundled directly inside Nexora; runtime remote font downloading is disabled.
  • No Ads or Trackers: Nexora contains zero third-party advertising networks, zero telemetry/analytics SDKs, and does not request Google Advertising ID (AD_ID).

4. Data Sharing, Retention & Your Controls

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies. You remain in complete control of your data:

  • Revoke Optional Permissions: Disable Accessibility Service, Notification Access, or Usage Access at any time in your Android device’s System Settings.
  • Reset Launcher Data: Clear saved layouts and preferences in Nexora Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Permanent Erasure: Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Nexora to permanently delete all locally stored settings, hidden-app lists, cached icons, and launch counts.

5. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Because Nexora processes personalization data strictly on your device (except user-initiated city weather lookups to Open-Meteo without personal identifiers):

  • EEA / UK Users (GDPR): Processing of local settings and optional permissions occurs solely on your explicit request to deliver launcher functionality. You can access, export (via Backup & Restore), or erase your data directly inside the app and Android Settings at any time.
  • California Users (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell personal information, do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not collect sensitive personal data for profiling.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Nexora is a general-audience Android utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal correspondence to our support email, contact us and we will promptly delete it.
