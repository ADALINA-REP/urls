Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I set Nexora as my default Android home screen?

Open Nexora Settings → Set as Default Launcher, or go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app and select Nexora.

Why are Accessibility, Notification, and Usage permissions optional?

Nexora runs cleanly without any special permissions. Each optional permission unlocks only its specific feature and stays strictly on-device:

  • Accessibility Service: Enables double-tap to lock screen and swipe-down to expand the notification shade (canRetrieveWindowContent="false").
  • Notification Access: Counts active notifications per app to draw unread numeric badges without reading message contents.
  • Usage Access: Improves Smart Dock & Suggested app ranking locally on your phone.
How does the Smart Dock choose apps?

By default, Nexora populates the dock with familiar essential apps (Phone, Messages, Browser, Camera, Photos) and ranks them dynamically as you launch apps from Nexora (or from Android Usage Stats if enabled). You can also customize your dock icons at any time.

How do I back up, restore, or reset my layout?

Go to Nexora Settings → Backup & Restore to export your homescreen layout, folders, and preferences as JSON text, import an existing backup, or reset your launcher configuration.

Does Nexora track me or show ads?

No. Nexora contains zero ad networks and zero analytics trackers. Only city search / forecasts for the optional Weather widget connect to Open-Meteo using your chosen city or coordinates.
