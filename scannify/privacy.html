🔒 Our Core Privacy Commitment

100% On-Device OCR Processing: Scannify scans, parses, and extracts receipt text (merchants, dates, amounts, taxes) entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). We do not operate a backend cloud server to read your financial documents. Your receipt images, personal receipts, and business tax calculations never leave your physical smartphone.

Zero Account Registration: You do not need to register an account, sign in with email, provide phone numbers, or link bank accounts to use Scannify.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ADALINA / SISCOM ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you install and use the mobile application Scannify: Receipt & Tax Scanner (referred to as "Scannify" or "the Application").

We built Scannify with a strict privacy-by-design and local-first architecture. We believe that your personal and business financial receipts, expense habits, and tax data belong exclusively to you.

2. Data Collected and How It Is Processed

Data Category Processed Locally Sent to Remote Servers Purpose & Retention
Receipt Images & Photos ✅ Yes (On-device OCR) ❌ No (Never transmitted) Optical character recognition to parse total amount, date, and tax. Stored only in app's private sandbox until deleted.

Advertising & Tracking SDKs ❌ None integrated ❌ No (Zero ad trackers) Scannify contains zero ad networks, zero advertising identifiers (no GAID/IDFA tracking), and zero third-party marketing SDKs.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

Scannify requests only the minimum permissions strictly required to perform receipt scanning:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA / iOS NSCameraUsageDescription): Required to scan physical paper receipts and invoices using your camera. Video frames are analyzed locally in device memory; no photos or video streams are ever transmitted to any external server.
  • Photo Library / Storage (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Optional permission requested only if you choose to import existing receipt photos or invoices from your gallery.

4. Zero Advertisements & Zero Ad Trackers

Scannify is built to be 100% ad-free, clean, and private. Unlike traditional expense management tools that monetize user attention or sell financial profiles:

  • No In-App Ads: There are no banner ads, interstitial popups, or rewarded video ads in Scannify.
  • No Ad SDKs: We do not include the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or any other third-party advertising SDK.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect, read, or transmit your Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • Unrestricted Features: All OCR scanning, deductible VAT computation, and audit-ready PDF/CSV exports are provided completely free and unrestricted.

5. Information We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Apple App Store Review Guidelines, we explicitly disclose that Scannify:

  • Does NOT access banking credentials: We never integrate bank APIs, Plaid, or automated credential scrapers.
  • Does NOT require personal identification: We do not ask for government IDs, SSN, credit cards, legal names, or dates of birth.
  • Does NOT track precise GPS location: The app does not request or access GPS or fine location coordinates.
  • Does NOT read contacts or personal communications: We have zero access to your address book, SMS, or emails.
  • Does NOT sell your data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your receipts or personal information to third-party data brokers.

6. Data Storage, Security & Permanent Deletion

App Sandboxing: All parsed receipts, expense items, and tax summaries are stored in an isolated, secure SQLite database inside the operating system's sandbox. No other applications on your device can access your Scannify data.

User Data Erasure ("Delete All Local Data"): You maintain complete sovereign control over your data. You can permanently wipe all receipts, scanned pictures, and database history at any moment by navigating to:
Settings > Privacy & About > Delete All Local Data.
Uninstalling the application from your device also immediately purges all application data and local files permanently.

7. Compliance with Global Privacy Frameworks

7.1 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EEA & UK)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act:

  • Data Controller & Processor: Because all financial processing occurs locally on your personal device, you are the sole controller of your financial records.
  • Legal Basis: Processing of device photos is performed strictly with your explicit consent and to deliver the contracted local OCR scanning service.
  • User Rights: You hold full rights to access, rectify, export (via CSV/PDF), and erase your data at any time directly inside the app.

7.2 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

For residents of California:

  • We do not sell personal financial information or receipt data.
  • We do not share your receipt information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • You have the right to request deletion of your information, which you can execute instantly in-app via the Delete All Local Data button.

7.3 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Scannify is designed for business professionals, freelancers, and general consumers managing expenses and tax deductible receipts. The Application is not directed toward children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
