Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How does Scannify read my receipts without uploading them?
Scannify utilizes high-performance on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) powered by Google ML Kit. All image analysis, vendor name extraction, dates, line items, and VAT/tax percentages run entirely on your phone's processor. Your receipts never touch a remote server, ensuring absolute privacy for your personal and company accounting.
Q. How do daily scans and free rewarded scans work?
Every user receives free scans daily that automatically reset every 24 hours. When you run out of daily free scans, you can watch an optional short rewarded video ad to instantly unlock additional free scans, keeping Scannify 100% free and functional without forcing a paid subscription.
Q. How do I export an Audit-Ready Accounting Dossier (PDF / CSV)?
Navigate to the Export tab in the bottom navigation bar. You can choose:
  • PDF Report: Generates a formal accounting dossier including financial summary, category breakdowns, tax breakdown, and an optional visual annex containing full-page copies of every physical receipt.
  • CSV Spreadsheet: Generates a clean tabular file ready to open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers, QuickBooks, or Xero.
Once generated, tap Share to send it via Email, Drive, WhatsApp, or AirDrop.
Q. How does deductible VAT / Tax tracking work?
When scanning a receipt, Scannify automatically detects standard VAT / Tax amounts printed on the receipt. You can also manually adjust or select the appropriate tax rate (e.g., 20%, 10%, 5.5%, or 0%) directly during the verification step. The Export module provides your exact total deductible VAT to streamline your quarterly or annual tax filing.
Q. How do I change the currency or application language?
Go to Settings (gear icon) > Currency & Language. Scannify supports 23 international currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, CAD, MAD, AED, SAR, etc.) and 7 languages (English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Arabic with full Right-to-Left RTL support).
Q. Tips for optimal receipt scanning accuracy
For the best recognition results:
  • Place the receipt on a flat, contrasting dark surface.
  • Ensure adequate, uniform lighting and avoid harsh shadows or camera flash glare.
  • Align the receipt vertically within the camera guidelines and hold the camera steady until the frame sharpens.
Q. How can I delete all my scanned receipts and data?
You have complete control over your data. Go to Settings > Privacy & About > tap Delete All Local Data. Confirming this action will immediately and irreversibly delete all stored receipts, cropped images, and category records from your device.
